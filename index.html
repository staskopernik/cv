About Me

I am a DevOps Engineer based in Lviv, Ukraine, with more than 12 years of experience in IT. My career began in technical support and system administration, where I built a strong foundation in Linux, Windows Server, networking, virtualization, security, backups and troubleshooting.

Today I focus on cloud infrastructure, CI/CD automation and Infrastructure as Code. I have practical experience with AWS and Azure, Jenkins and GitLab CI/CD, Terraform, Ansible/AWX, Docker, Kubernetes, JFrog Artifactory, monitoring and centralized logging. I have supported multi-project environments and applications written primarily in Python and C#.

I am self-motivated, responsible and comfortable working independently or as part of a team. I enjoy solving infrastructure problems, improving delivery processes and creating maintainable automation.
